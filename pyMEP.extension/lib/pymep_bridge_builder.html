Deck
The deck fits inside the four posts, centred on their midpoints, and slopes from the start post bases to the end post bases. Its thickness comes from the floor type.
Under the deck
Cross beams and bracing have their TOP on the floor underside (z justification Top). Stiffeners are hosted on the floor's underside, running along the span between the cross beams.
Railing
Hosted on the deck along both edges. "(none)" places no railing.
Member types structural framing
Top and bottom chords can take different sections. Types come from the model - load the framing families before opening the builder.
